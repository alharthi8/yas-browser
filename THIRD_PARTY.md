# Third-party components

- **Wry** 0.57.1 and **Tao** 0.37.2: Tauri project, MIT/Apache-2.0. https://github.com/tauri-apps/wry and https://github.com/tauri-apps/tao
- **adblock-rust** 0.13.5: Brave authors, Mozilla Public License 2.0. https://github.com/brave/adblock-rust . Yas uses the crate without source modifications. Exact source is available from https://crates.io/api/v1/crates/adblock/0.13.5/download . The MPL license text is bundled in `licenses/MPL-2.0.txt`.
- **EasyList** and **EasyPrivacy**: The EasyList authors (https://easylist.to/). Unmodified snapshots dated 8 November 2026 are in `filters/`, preserving source headers. Distributed under Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 3.0 Unported (one of the authors' dual-license options). License: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/ ; policy: https://easylist.to/pages/licence.html . Source: https://github.com/easylist/easylist . Full CC license text is in `licenses/CC-BY-SA-3.0.txt`.
- **Dubai** typeface: used from the operating system; not redistributed.
- **Microsoft Edge WebView2**: the full runtime is installed separately. A Microsoft-signed Evergreen bootstrapper is included under `prerequisites/` in portable/installed packages; provenance and SHA256 are recorded there. It requires internet to download the runtime, and setup asks before running it. https://developer.microsoft.com/microsoft-edge/webview2/
- **Microsoft Visual C++ Runtime**: unmodified, Microsoft-signed x64 `vcruntime140.dll` and `vcruntime140_1.dll` app-local redistributable binaries from Visual Studio's Redist directory. Included adjacent to the application in installer/portable packages, retaining Microsoft's embedded copyright and signature. https://learn.microsoft.com/cpp/windows/redistributing-visual-cpp-files

- **redb** 4.5.0: Christopher Berner and contributors, MIT OR Apache-2.0. Unmodified crate source: https://crates.io/api/v1/crates/redb/4.5.0/download ; repository: https://github.com/cberner/redb . Both license texts are in `licenses/dependencies/redb-4.5.0/`.
- **postcard** 1.1.3: James Munns and contributors, MIT OR Apache-2.0. Unmodified crate source: https://crates.io/api/v1/crates/postcard/1.1.3/download ; repository: https://github.com/jamesmunns/postcard . Both license texts are in `licenses/dependencies/postcard-1.1.3/`.
- **cobs** 0.3.0 and **embedded-io** 0.4.0 / 0.6.1: MIT OR Apache-2.0. Exact crate license texts accompany the source in their respective `licenses/dependencies/` directories.

- **Ollama** 0.40.16 local AI runtime: Ollama and contributors, MIT. The compact package embeds an unmodified CPU-only subset of the official Windows AMD64 runtime; provenance, the vendor archive checksum and per-file hashes are in `ai-runtime/AI_RUNTIME_MANIFEST.json`. The MIT text is `ai-runtime/OLLAMA_LICENSE.txt`; component notices are `ai-runtime/lib/ollama/LLAMA_CPP_LICENSE`, `LLAMA_CPP_VENDORS_LICENSE`, `CPP_HTTPLIB_LICENSE` and `GO_LICENSE`. Official source and release: https://github.com/ollama/ollama/tree/v0.40.16 . GPU runtime packages are not included.
- **Qwen3 0.6B** optional local model: Qwen Team, Apache-2.0. The compact installer does not contain model weights. First-use setup downloads the pinned `qwen3:0.6b` model (522,653,767 bytes of registry layers); its manifest digest and source are recorded in `ai-runtime/AI_RUNTIME_MANIFEST.json`. The model license is `ai-runtime/QWEN3_LICENSE.txt`. Official model: https://huggingface.co/Qwen/Qwen3-0.6B ; distribution: https://ollama.com/library/qwen3:0.6b . Gemma model weights are not redistributed.

Other Rust dependencies and exact versions are recorded in `Cargo.lock`. Bundled texts and attributions for the Windows normal/build dependency graph accompany the source distribution in `licenses/dependencies/`.

The dependency index includes all non-root packages in the locked Cargo graph. `licenses/dependencies/provenance049.json` records the Windows normal/build attribution coverage, package checksums for the new Rust storage crates, and exact upstream sources used to complete older missing texts. SeaHash 4.1.0 publishes an MIT declaration without a standalone license file; its authorship metadata and a clearly labeled canonical SPDX MIT text are bundled.
