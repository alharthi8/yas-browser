Downloads in the background

Closing the window keeps unfinished regular downloads running in the system tray. Open Yas again to return to Downloads.

Off by default. When enabled, the Stealth session and its cookies stay alive until those downloads end. Close all Stealth tabs or choose Exit Yas in the tray menu to end that session sooner.

Exit Yas stops unfinished downloads. After Yas fully exits or your PC restarts, start a new download from the website. Completed regular downloads remain listed here; Stealth download records are not saved.

Light by design.

Up to two website views stay loaded, plus tabs with active or paused downloads. Inactive tabs release their page memory after 2 minutes. Restored tabs load when selected.

Sleeping reloads the page when you return. Unsaved form input, page playback, and per-tab back/forward state can be lost. Active, paused, or resumable downloads keep their tabs awake. Closing their tab cancels those transfers. Memory use depends on the websites and extensions you run.
